Notice for External Server Administrators & Community Creators:
Direct store catalog publishing is reserved exclusively for the FiestaSMP core team to keep all servers safe from unvetted or broken code.

How to publish your addon to the official FiestaPanel store:
  1. Export your addon bundle as a .zip package from your dashboard.
  2. Submit a Pull Request to our open-source GitHub repository or send your .zip file directly to our team on Discord.
  3. The FiestaSMP core team will review, security-audit, test, and approve the sent .zip file before publishing it to the official in-panel plugin store catalog!
